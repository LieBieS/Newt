# Brand Briefing PDF

## 1. Executive Summary (1 paragraph)
- Company name
- What we do in one sentence
- Market opportunity size
- Why now

## 2. The Problem (2 paragraphs)
- Who experiences this pain
- Current alternatives and their flaws
- Cost of inaction

## 3. Our Solution (2 paragraphs)
- Product/service summary
- Core differentiator
- Competitive advantage

## 4. Brand & Positioning (1 paragraph)
- Archetype
- Target customer
- Pricing tier

## 5. Traction & Metrics (1 paragraph)
- Revenue / user growth / key milestones
- Timeline to next milestone

## 5. The Team (optional, 1 paragraph)
- Founder / key hires relevant to the market

## 6. The Ask (1 paragraph)
- Funding required
- Use of funds
- Milestone timeline
