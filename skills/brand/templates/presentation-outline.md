# Presentation Outline

## 1. Opening (2 minutes)
- Brand mission in one sentence
- Why this moment matters now

## 2. The Problem (5 minutes)
- Market gap quantified
- Customer pain (verbatim if available)
- Why existing solutions fall short

## 2. The Brand (5 minutes)
- Archetype and why it matters
- Core values in action
- Unique value proposition

## 3. The Solution (10 minutes)
- Product/service overview
- Key differentiators (2x2 matrix position)
- Go-to-market approach

## 4. Social Proof (5 minutes)
- Customer logos or quotes
- Case study highlight
- Metrics (revenue, growth, retention)

## 5. The Ask (3 minutes)
- What you need (funding, partnership, talent)
- Milestone timeline
- Use of funds / impact

## 6. Closing (2 minutes)
- Brand mission recap
- Next steps contact info
